## A. RiftSketch, level "sketch" (heroes, Match Day inset, share strip, OG)

- **Technique:** the T1 sheet from measured coordinates. Frame (four strokes, open corners), 3 lanes, river, 2 bases, impassable terrain as hatch, Baron/Dragon diamonds with words, a few bush clusters. Inlined into the SSR HTML (generated Rust `const` via `include_str!`, <= 4 KB), not fetched: first paint carries the picture (answers R0b's "injects after fetch" caveat).
- **Smoothing (decided):** walls are **never drawn as outlines at this level**; they exist only as hatch masks. Mask = survey wall rings, Douglas-Peucker epsilon **2.0 u** (300 game units), rings with area < 8 u² or narrower than 4 u dropped (hatch would be < 3 u), then merged where rings are < 2 u apart. **<= 14 vertices per ring, <= 60 in all**, no curve fitting (straight chords; the edge is invisible anyway). Why: an outline at 14 vertices reads as a blob (sticker); lines ending read as terrain (T1-ref grey layer: tone from density, no edge).
- **Lanes:** each = hand-placed polyline of <= 4 vertices through the measured lane centreline, snapped to axis-aligned if the deviation is <= 1.5 u (R0 T1-1: built things ruled). Mid = one straight segment between the bases (open ends 3 u short). Open ends at the bases stay.
- **River:** the two R1-a cubics (7 control points); keep them unless the measured centreline deviates > 1.5 u anywhere, then refit with the same 2 cubics (never more). Banks +-1.5 u, 48 samples per cubic, broken where within 2 u of mid (bridge gap, inset 5 u).
- **Bases:** blue = circle r3, red = square 6, centred on the measured nexus positions (replace R0's (12,88) and (88,12) if they differ; expect +-2 u). Side shapes in ink, no side colour.
- **Hatch (replaces the quadrant hatch):** axis-aligned only (vertical in the top and bottom quadrants, horizontal left and right, as R0), pitch **6 u**, clipped to the mask inset **1.5 u**, run length 3 to 16 u, seeded +-0.6 u jitter on the inner end (seed 151, as R0), clear of label and mark boxes and 2.5 u around bases. Target **4.5 to 6.0 % lit pixels** in the 560 box (R0 measured 5.1 to 5.5 %; `critique-r0b-t1.md` ruling). If out of range tune **pitch only (5 to 7 u)**, never stroke or opacity.
- **Features that appear:** lanes, river, bases, impassable hatch, Baron and Dragon (diamond 2.9 u + `eyebrow`-size 12 px `muted` word, away from the river; both widths), **bush clusters** for the 10 largest bushes only (3 dots r1 px, triangle, 5 px apart, at the centroid: points, not outlines). **Towers, inhibitors, camps: not at this level.** Reason: R0b already flagged hatch stubs reading as ruler ticks; 12 tower ticks would repeat it and spend the 6 % budget; they appear at survey level.
- **Insets and crops:** *Match Day inset* 88 px: no frame, no hatch, no labels, no bush, no diamonds; lanes, river with the gap widened to `mid_dist < 5` (R0b optional, take it), bases. *Share strip 358x96* (viewBox 40 x 10.7 u, 8.95 px/u; 1280: 640x120, 50 x 9.4 u): **no hatch (R1-b), no bush**; lane + river or base only. **Crop rule:** centre on a base only if the shared artefact carries a side (then the label names that base, right-aligned, leader 7 u); otherwise centre on the river/mid bridge at (50,50) with no label. No team name beyond what the page already prints. Never both bases in the crop. *Player-card share*: bridge crop.
- **Do not:** draw wall outlines at this level; fit curves to walls; add a second hatch angle; towers as ticks; side colour; jitter other than the hatch ends; any pixel from `map11.png` anywhere in the build.
- **Reduced motion:** n/a, static always. **Verify:** stills 390x852 and 1280x800 at DPR 1 and 2; lit-pixel % in the sketch box; contrast from pixels (ink >= 8.0 on `page`); a script check that every sketch vertex lies within 1.5 u of survey geometry; side by side with T1-ref `map-grey.png`; one 4x nearest-neighbour crop of the 1x inset (hatch tone even, gap visible).

## B. RiftSketch, level "survey" (`/design` preview only, three stops 1x / 2x / 4x)

- **Technique:** exact measured outlines (the committed polygons), same renderer, same stroke. Switch: stops <= 1x render sketch, >= 2x render survey; instant swap, no crossfade; stops are a `SegmentedControl` (`manual`), no wheel, no drag-pan this round (M149/R2 owns the zoomable map).
- **Walls:** closed outline 1 px ink + 45-degree hatch inside, **6 px screen pitch at every stop** (game-unit pitch 160 / 80 / 40 at a 560 px square), regenerated per stop; hatch ends 4 px inside the outline. (Different angle from the sketch's axis-aligned hatch on purpose: survey is "drawn to the survey", the sketch is "sketched".)
- **Bushes:** outline 1 px ink, **no hatch**, interior **stipple**: dots of 2 px (zero-length round-cap paths) on a 7 px hex grid, clipped inside. Distinct from walls by class (dots vs lines, T1-ref grey layer), same ink, legible at 1x (2 px dots at 8:1).
- **Structures (decided: ink glyphs we draw, not the CommunityDragon PNGs):** a recoloured RGBA icon is an alpha silhouette, which turns into a blob and breaks "one stroke"; and the Rift stays an *original* schematic (mission decision). Glyphs follow DESIGN 6.9 shapes: tower = filled dot r2.5 px; inhibitor = hollow diamond 9 px; nexus = side shape 16 px (circle blue, square red) with a 4 px centre dot; Baron/Elder pit = hollow diamond 14 px; Herald/Grubs pit = hollow diamond 10 px; buff camp = ring r3.5 + dot; small camp = ring r2 px. Neutral ink, no side colour, no chalk. The real icons appear beside *words* only (card C, objective lists).
- **Elevation stub:** river basin polygon tagged `"elev": -1`, everything else 0; **no contour rendering**. Finding to write in `docs/rift-schematic.md`: the Rift has one grade (basin), so a contour is one closed line that the river banks already draw; contours would duplicate them, so none.
- **Budget:** <= 150 paths, <= 3,000 vertices, geometry JSON <= 40 KB gzip; renders < 16 ms per stop.
- **Do not:** hatch bushes; tone-fill walls; icons as `<img>` on the drawing; labels except Baron/Dragon words; contours. **Verify:** stills at each stop, 4x crop of a bush and a wall at 1x, count of drawn objects against the JSON, side by side with `map-grey.png` (hachure vs stipple read as two things at arm's length).

Rows are `flex-nowrap`, never `flex-wrap`; widths are computed, not left to the browser. e2e at 320 / 390 / 1280: five icons per team share one `offsetTop` (row) or one `offsetLeft` (column).
- **Signature:** the sketch with its one chalk label. No draw-on, no hover effect. **Do not:** a hero-plus-three-cards band, a pill above the headline, a gradient, champion art behind text, a second chalk item, a counter.
