## Against card T3 (V8)

- Canvas ground (2..98) framed by a 1 px `rule` square, the only stroke in the figure; no outlines on shapes, one fill per element, no gradient, blur, shadow or texture.
- Jungle: four triangular quadrants in `terrain-jungle`, inset 1.5 units from every lane, the river and the bases (canvas verges), sharp apexes at the crossing.
- River: an 8-unit band on y = x in `terrain-river`, ends cut square to the lane box; mid crosses over it (as T1 breaks the river for mid). Signature: the widest, only blue-hued band.
- Lanes: 4-unit bands in `terrain-lane`: top and bot as L-shapes with mitred elbows (centre lines 10 / 90), mid on the diagonal from base to base.
- Bases: circle r5 at (10,90) `side-blue-deep`, 9-unit square centred (90,10) `side-red-deep`.
- Text never on terrain: the chalk label ("YOUR TEAM", share "RIVERSIDE ROOKS") and the 1280 mark words (Baron, Dragon at T1's spots) carry a `canvas` halo (t3.css, scoped to `[data-t="t3"]`). Ticks A-H / 1-5 sit outside the frame (1280 only).

## Calls I made (for the critique)

1. **Inset bases neutral:** the field card forbids side colours in the Match Day inset, so there both bases fill `terrain-lane`; circle vs square still tells the sides.
2. **Mark halos:** the marks keep T1's spots inside jungle quadrants and get a canvas plate rather than a carved clearing (a clearing would show as an empty window at 390, where marks are hidden). They read as small dark tags; the alternative is moving Baron/Dragon onto the river band's edge.
3. **Lane over river** at the mid crossing, so the river reads broken there; drawing the river on top would make the signature continuous at the cost of the lane.
4. **Low ground contrast** is by token (jungle on canvas is barely visible at 88 px); no new colour added.
