## Calls I made (for the critique)

1. **Ring spacing.** The card's "6 to 8 nested closed curves, 12 units apart" does not fit a 100-unit field (8 closed rings at 12 units need about 190 units). Rings are about 5.5 units apart across the river and about 8 along it (ellipses 1.5:1 along the diagonal), all closed. At 12 units the field read as a target.
2. **390 field.** The skeleton's hero slot is square; `t2.css` clips it to the card's 358x240 (the square's middle band, y 16.48 to 83.52, by `clip-path` and negative % margins) so the overlay anchors stay right. The band crops the top and bottom of the outer rings: an excerpt of the sheet, not closed in view.
3. **Index break per scale.** The figure is 12 px everywhere, so the break is written three times: `v-w` (5.6 px/unit: hero from 1024 px, and the share crop chosen at the same scale), `v-n` (358 px field), `v-0` unbroken for the inset. A hero between 391 and 1023 px wide keeps the 358 break (slightly loose gap).
4. **Share strip.** Crop over the top flank with the "400" figure; "RIVERSIDE ROOKS" chalk on the red (upper-right) half with a `canvas` halo, as T1's call 2.
5. **Match Day inset.** A 48-unit crop of the two pits and the saddle, ground `canvas` (per the card) inside the `ink-1` card, no figures, no marks, no chalk.
