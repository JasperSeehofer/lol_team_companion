# M160 item 10 — One lesson pipeline (judgment, privacy boundary)
Privacy rule (hard): a personal learning is visible ONLY to its owner. It becomes team-visible ONLY by the owner's explicit "Promote to pool note" click, as a COPY into a new champion_note owned by that user. The draft rail shows ONLY the viewer's own learnings, labelled "Only you see this". Never another member's learnings, never to a coach. No new stored field.
