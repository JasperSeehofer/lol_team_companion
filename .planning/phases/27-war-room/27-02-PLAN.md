<objective>
Add a `static AtomicBool` accumulation guard to `AtmosphereRoot` so navigating between rooms cannot leave two simultaneous `mousemove` listeners on `window` (D-08, Pitfall 5, Leptos #3665). This is the second foundation change: the War Room is the first time two `AtmosphereRoot` instances can coexist in the router (War Room ↔ Solo Journey / flat-route round-trips), so the guard becomes load-bearing now.

Purpose: Leptos `on_cleanup` may not fire atomically on the first navigation under the same router parent. Without a guard, a War Room → Solo Journey → War Room round-trip can register two `mousemove` handlers, doubling parallax amplitude. The existing `on_cleanup` listener removal stays; the AtomicBool is insurance that satisfies Success Criterion 4.

Output: `parallax.rs` with a module-level `static MOUSEMOVE_ACTIVE: AtomicBool`, a `compare_exchange(false,true)` early-return gate before listener registration (inside the hydrate block), and a `store(false)` reset in `on_cleanup`.
</objective>
