<objective>
Refactor the Phase 26 `RoomSurface` component from `<Show>`-gated unmount/remount panels to keep-mounted, CSS-hidden panels with a per-tab render contract (D-06, D-07, D-09). This is the gating foundation change: `WarSurfaces` (Wave 3) depends on the new contract, and keep-mounted panels are what guarantee Success Criterion 1 (zero atmosphere flash on tab switch).

Purpose: War Room is `RoomSurface`'s first real tabbed consumer. The old single-`ChildrenFn` API forced every panel to render the same children and branch on active-tab context; five distinct surfaces each need their own independent content. Keep-mounted panels also persist in-tab state across tab switches and eliminate mount churn that could flash the parallax atmosphere.

Output: Updated `room_surface.rs` with the new `panels: Vec<(SurfaceTab, PanelFn)>` signature, a `PanelFn` type alias, keep-mounted `attr:hidden` panels, and an updated dummy-surface render test. The four pure-function `resolve_active_tab` unit tests survive unchanged.
</objective>
