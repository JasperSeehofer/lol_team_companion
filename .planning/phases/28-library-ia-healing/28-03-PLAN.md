<objective>
Extract the chrome-free `StatsSurface` (from `stats.rs`) and `MatchDetailSurface` (from `match_detail.rs`), and wire the Stats→Match-Detail bridge (D-07): the Stats match-row CTA stops linking to `/match/:id` and instead sets a shared `RwSignal<Option<String>>` and navigates to `?tab=match`; `MatchDetailSurface` derives its active match id from that shared signal with a `?id=` query-param fallback, and shows an in-tab recent-match list when nothing is selected (D-06). This is the one genuinely new design problem in the phase, so the two bridged surfaces are extracted together in a single plan.

Purpose: These two surfaces are the only pair that share runtime state. Extracting them together keeps the shared-context contract (`selected_match_id`) coherent. The other three surfaces (Analytics, Post-Game, Personal Learnings) are independent and extracted in Plan 04.

Output: `StatsSurface` + `MatchDetailSurface` components, each chrome-free per the D-04 recipe; the Stats CTA rewired to the shared signal; `MatchDetailSurface` self-contained with a recent-match fallback list. Both compile targets pass.
</objective>
